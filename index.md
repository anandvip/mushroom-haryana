# Mushroom Farming in Haryana: A Benchmark Study of the Full Cycle

**Focus area:** Kurukshetra district (Thanesar and Pehowa areas), with neighbouring Karnal, Yamunanagar, Panipat and Sonipat, plus state-wide figures.
**Research date:** 10 November 2026 (all "today" prices are as reported on or before this date).

> **How to read this study.** Every claim has a numbered source [S#] with the date of the figure. Anything I could not check against a primary or reliable source is marked **Unverified**. Where public data does not exist, the study says so. Prices change daily, so treat them as reference points, not quotes. This study is general information. It is not financial or investment advice.

---

## 1. Summary

- **Haryana is a mid-to-large producer, and the official figures disagree.** The Haryana Horticulture Department's district table for 2023 totals **12,718 t** [S1]. A 2023 HAU statement put the state **third in India at 21,500 t** [S2]. The national Final Estimates for 2024-25 (as compiled by a third-party data site) give **23.8 thousand t, 7th in India** [S3]. Use the district table to compare districts, not to state the total.
- **Sonipat leads, and Kurukshetra is the fastest-rising cluster near the focus area.** In 2023 the leading districts were Sonipat (4,600 t), Panipat (2,135 t), Yamunanagar (1,787 t), Karnal (900 t) and Kurukshetra (788 t) [S1]. By July 2026, Kurukshetra's District Horticulture Officer was quoting **over 1,875 t a year** from about **200 commercial growers**, mostly in **Pehowa block**, with **17 AC units and 181 seasonal (non-AC) units** [S4].
- **Button mushroom is grown almost everywhere.** In Kurukshetra "almost all" commercial growers grow button. One grower each was reported for oyster and milky [S4]. Oyster, milky, shiitake and other types are niche crops, sold mostly by trainers and specialty sellers [S5][S20].
- **Training is cheap or free in Haryana and paid at ICAR-DMR Solan.** HAU and its KVKs (farm science centres) run free 5-day courses [S6][S7]. The Horticulture Department runs free 5-day courses at Sunderah (Mahendragarh) [S8]. ICAR-DMR Solan charges ₹4,500–₹15,000 [S9].
- **Mandi prices (Agmarknet, via data aggregators):** Thanesar APMC, the market yard in Kurukshetra, paid a modal (most common) price of **₹150/kg (₹140–160) on 9 Nov 2026**. Ladwa APMC paid **₹230/kg on 5 Nov 2026**. Azadpur (Delhi) paid **₹110/kg (₹80–140)** on the same day [S10]. One Sonipat grower reports realising **₹80/kg in winter and ₹180/kg in summer, about ₹120/kg on average** [S11].
- **Subsidy:** under MIDH, the national horticulture mission run in Haryana by the Horticulture Department, private units get **40% of a cost norm** (a fixed reference cost). The norms are ₹30 lakh for a production unit, ₹20 lakh for a spawn lab and ₹30 lakh for a compost unit, paid as a credit-linked, back-ended subsidy. A **low-cost 200 sq ft unit** has a norm of ₹2 lakh with 50% assistance, for up to 5 units [S12].
- **Biggest gaps:** Agmarknet does not publish any farm-gate (buying) price series by tehsil. JustDial and Google Maps counts could not be pulled. I found no published "mushroom village" census.

## 10. The complete cycle, with indicative costs

| Stage | What happens (button mushroom) | Key numbers | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Compost | Wheat/paddy straw + urea, superphosphate/gypsum, bran, water. **Short method:** ~12 days outdoors (Phase I) + pasteurisation at 60 °C for 4 h. **Long method:** ~28 days, 7–8 turnings. Bajwa: 30 days by the long method, 15 days with a turner | C:N 25–30:1 at stacking, 16–17:1 final; 70% moisture, pH 7.5 | [S29], [S15] |
| 2. Spawning | Mix spawn through the compost | 0.5–0.75% (500–750 g per 100 kg compost); Sonipat practice is 1 kg spawn for 100 bags | [S29], [S11] |
| 3. Spawn run | Bags (20–25 kg) or trays | 12–14 days at 23 ± 2 °C, ~90% RH. Bajwa: 15 days at 25–27 °C, 10 kg bags | [S29], [S15] |
| 4. Casing | 3–4 cm layer of pasteurised soil/FYM/spent compost | Pasteurise at 66–70 °C for 7–8 h; pH 7–7.5 | [S29] |
| 5. Cropping | Pinheads → buttons | 23 °C for a week, then 16 ± 2 °C; RH >85%; CO₂ 0.08–0.15% | [S29] |
| 6. Harvest | Pick by twisting at 2.5–4 cm cap; first flush about 3 weeks after casing; crop lasts about 8–10 weeks | 10–14 kg per 100 kg compost (long method); 15–20 kg (short method). Sonipat: ~2 kg per bag; Bajwa: 2–2.5 kg per 10 kg compost | [S29], [S32], [S11], [S15] |
| 7. Post-harvest | Wash in KMS solution (5 g per 10 L), pack 250–500 g perforated bags, store at 4–5 °C for 3–4 days; can, IQF or pickle for longer storage | — | [S29] |
| 8. Marketing | Mandi / Azadpur, interstate traders, direct, canning (section 5) | Winter glut, Dec–Feb | [S29], [S4] |

## 11. What could not be verified (and data gaps)

1. **The state total is inconsistent:** 12,718 t (Hort Dept 2023) vs 21,500 t (HAU, about 2023) vs 23.8 kt (2024-25 Final Estimates via the third-party site indianagri.com). I could not open the primary DA&FW workbook for 2024-25.
2. **Kurukshetra output:** 788 t (2023 Hort Dept table) vs "over 1,875 MT" (DHO, Jul 2026). This may be real growth or a different way of counting. **Unverified.**
