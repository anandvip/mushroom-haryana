1. Summary

  • Haryana is a mid-to-large producer, and the official figures disagree. The Haryana Horticulture Department's district table for 2023 totals 12,718 t [S1] flag. A 2023 HAU statement put the state third in India at 21,500 t [S2] flag. The national Final Estimates for 2024-25 (as compiled by a third-party data site) give 23.8 thousand t, 7th in India [S3] flag. Use the district table to compare districts, not to state the total.
  • Sonipat leads, and Kurukshetra is the fastest-rising cluster near the focus area. In 2023 the leading districts were Sonipat (4,600 t), Panipat (2,135 t), Yamunanagar (1,787 t), Karnal (900 t) and Kurukshetra (788 t) [S1] fixed. By July 2026, Kurukshetra's District Horticulture Officer was quoting over 1,875 t a year from about 200 commercial growers, mostly in Pehowa block, with 17 AC units and 181 seasonal (non-AC) units [S4] fixed. The 2026 figure is about 2.4 times the 2023 one (1,875 ÷ 788 = 2.38) derived, but the two may count differently flag.
  • Button mushroom is grown almost everywhere. In Kurukshetra "almost all" commercial growers grow button. One grower each was reported for oyster and milky [S4] fixed. Oyster, milky, shiitake and other types are niche crops, sold mostly by trainers and specialty sellers [S5][S20] fixed.
  • Training is cheap or free in Haryana and paid at ICAR-DMR Solan. HAU and its KVKs (farm science centres) run free 5-day courses [S6][S7] fixed. The Horticulture Department runs free 5-day courses at Sunderah (Mahendragarh) [S8] fixed. ICAR-DMR Solan charges ₹4,500–₹15,000 [S9] flag (reproduction domain).
  • Mandi prices (Agmarknet, via data aggregators): Thanesar APMC, the market yard in Kurukshetra, paid a modal (most common) price of ₹150/kg (₹140–160) on 9 Nov 2026. Ladwa APMC paid ₹230/kg on 5 Nov 2026. Azadpur (Delhi) paid ₹110/kg (₹80–140) on the same day [S10] fixed. One Sonipat grower reports realising ₹80/kg in winter and ₹180/kg in summer, about ₹120/kg on average [S11] fixed. Update: the latest Haryana reading is ₹200/kg modal (₹180–220) on 21 Nov 2026, from the single reporting Kurukshetra market [S39] revised; see §14.
  • Subsidy: under MIDH, the national horticulture mission run in Haryana by the Horticulture Department, private units get 40% of a cost norm (a fixed reference cost). The norms are ₹30 lakh for a production unit, ₹20 lakh for a spawn lab and ₹30 lakh for a compost unit, paid as a credit-linked, back-ended subsidy. A low-cost 200 sq ft unit has a norm of ₹2 lakh with 50% assistance, for up to 5 units [S12] fixed. That is at most ₹12 lakh per production or compost unit and ₹8 lakh per spawn lab (§12) derived.
  • Biggest gaps: Agmarknet does not publish any farm-gate (buying) price series by tehsil. JustDial and Google Maps counts could not be pulled. I found no published "mushroom village" census.

Stage What happens (button mushroom) Key numbers Source Status
1. Compost Wheat/paddy straw + urea, superphosphate/gypsum, bran, water. Short method: ~12 days outdoors (Phase I) + pasteurisation at 60 °C for 4 h. Long method: ~28 days, 7–8 turnings. Bajwa: 30 days by the long method, 15 days with a turner C:N 25–30:1 at stacking, 16–17:1 final; 70% moisture, pH 7.5 [S29], [S15] fixed

Button mushroom in the Haryana plains is a cool-season crop in seasonal huts; AC rooms extend it year-round (§1). The month-by-month steps below come from ICAR-DMR's 2026 crop advisory [S40] and one Hisar hut grower's account [S51].

13. Diseases and risks (new)

RiskWhat the source saysSourceStatus
Main diseasesWet bubble, dry bubble, cobweb and moulds (green, yellow, brown)[S44]fixed
Dry bubble (Verticillium fungicola)"Most common and serious" fungal disease; uncontrolled, it can destroy a crop in 2–3 weeks. Best at 20 °C; favoured by high humidity, poor air circulation, late picking and temperatures above 16 °C; more common when cropping runs past 61 days. The pathogen was found in compost and casing samples from farms in Haryana, HP and Punjab.   1. The state total is inconsistent: 12,718 t (Hort Dept 2023) vs 21,500 t (HAU, about 2023) vs 23.8 kt (2024-25 Final Estimates via the third-party site indianagri.com). I could not open the primary DA&FW workbook for 2024-25.

  6. The NHB 21 Aug 2026 circular cutting NHB subsidy to 35%: reported by an aggregator only.

  8. Academic cost studies [S36], [S37]: the full texts were not accessible.
  9. The Barara ₹35/kg reading: a possible data error.
